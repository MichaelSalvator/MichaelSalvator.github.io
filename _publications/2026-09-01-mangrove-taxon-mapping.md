Mapping mangrove taxa from satellite data is difficult because species are spectrally similar and training labels are scarce. This work combines Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 features in a compact one-dimensional network, calibrates the output probabilities, uses predictive entropy to flag unreliable predictions, and evaluates transfer to unseen regions with frozen backbones. **Third author. Accepted.**
