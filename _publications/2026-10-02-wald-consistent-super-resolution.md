The 20 m red-edge and SWIR bands of Sentinel-2 are the most informative for vegetation and moisture, yet they are the coarsest. This work sharpens them to 10 m using structure borrowed from the native 10 m NIR band and enforces Wald consistency so that the enhanced bands remain usable for quantitative retrieval.
