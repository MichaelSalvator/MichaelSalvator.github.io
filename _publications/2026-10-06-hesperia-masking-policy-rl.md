Random masking is a weak pretext task for hyperspectral imagery because the informative content is concentrated in a few diagnostic bands. HESPERIA treats masking as a sequential decision problem and learns the policy with deep reinforcement learning, so that pretraining focuses on the spectral regions that carry the class signal. **First author.**
