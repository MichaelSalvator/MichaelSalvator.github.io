Satellite-derived bathymetry in optically complex coastal waters is limited by the nonlinear relationship between water-leaving reflectance and depth. This work applies a Transformer model to Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery and shows that self-attention over spectral bands improves depth retrieval compared with conventional regression and convolutional baselines. **First author.**
